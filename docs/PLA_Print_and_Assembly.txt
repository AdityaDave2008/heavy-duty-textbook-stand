KNIGHT PLA STAND - MATERIAL-SAVING V3

WHAT CHANGED
The two cradles now use 40 mm-wide inner triangular frames, with 14 mm-thick
backrest rails around the original connector sockets. Full-width 6 mm back
panels support the covers. The full-width shelf has a 10 mm-thick outer wing
and an added underside gusset. A transverse heel foot supports the outer wing.
The base and rear brace no longer extend across the whole 130 mm cradle width.
This removes 49.57% of each cradle's CAD volume compared with compact V2:
  V2: 1,133.35 cm3 per cradle
  V3:   571.53 cm3 per cradle
This is a GEOMETRY comparison, not a measured filament or print-time saving.
Creality Print must calculate grams and hours using your actual printer profile.
No slicer, printer, load test, or FEA was run here. The 8 lb target is unverified.

WHAT STAYS THE SAME
Cradle size: 185.933 x 190 x 130 mm in supplied print orientation.
Reading angle: 70 degrees. Backrest length: 180 mm. Assembled width: 440 mm.
Center gap: 180 mm. Clear shelf depth: 76 mm. Connector heights: 50 and 145 mm.
Both connector socket profiles and all seven page-finger fit positions remain.
03_Connector_Bar_PRINT_2.stl and 04_Page_Finger_PRINT_2.stl are byte-identical
to the previous version. Reuse your existing printed bars and fingers.
Compact V2 cradles use the same connection locations as V3. Already printed
V2 cradles do not need replacing solely to save filament. Use V3 for unprinted
pieces. Do not mix these with the original tall cradles, whose upper joint was
190 mm high.

IMPORTANT: NEW PRINT ORIENTATION
Use the supplied V3 STLs or 3MFs exactly as oriented. Each cradle prints on
its broad INNER frame side, with the thinner outer wing higher above the bed.
Do not flip it over to match the older files. This keeps the main triangular
frame in the layer planes and avoids adding unsupported frame sections later.
The two connector pocket roofs have 45-degree reliefs so they close gradually
without a broad bridge. The reliefs start beyond the original key clearance.
The keeper socket roofs still require short 6.6 mm bridges. Inspect these in
sliced Preview. Print the new 00A socket coupon first; it matches the inner-
face-down orientation and roof slope. Fit your actual connector to this coupon.
Supports off initially, with a 5 mm brim. Check the coupon roof and joint fit
before committing to a large cradle.

CREALITY PRINT STARTING SETTINGS (0.4 MM NOZZLE, ORDINARY PLA)
CRADLES 01 AND 02:
  Layer height: 0.24 mm. First layer: 0.20 mm.
  Walls: 6. Top layers: 7. Bottom layers: 7 (about 1.68 mm solid skins).
  Infill: 25% gyroid. Brim: 5 mm. Scale: 100%, millimeter units.
  Keep a calibrated PLA structural/normal profile and its bridge settings.
  Use the filament maker's temperature range. Do not use a draft/racing profile.
BARS 03 AND FINGERS 04:
  Keep the previous 0.20 mm layers, 6 walls, 100% infill, and orientation.
FIT COUPON 00A:
  Use the new cradle layer, wall and bridge settings. Fit it with the actual
  final connector bar. The optional whole coupon plate can use 0.24 mm layers
  and 100% infill; its small-part copies are for fit checks only. Print the
  final load-carrying bars and fingers with their settings above.
These settings are a starting recipe, not a certified load capacity.
Keeping six walls preserves the external shells while reducing sparse infill.
0.24 mm layers reduce the cradle layer count from about 650 to 542; actual
print time does not decrease in direct proportion to layer count or CAD volume.

FREECAD
The updated Knight_PLA_Stand.FCMacro builds V3. In Macro > Macros, select the
extracted folder, select the macro, and Execute. Choose an output folder.
It writes individual native FCStd, STEP and STL files in a timestamped folder.
It uses FreeCAD's built-in modules and creates editable base solids, not a
fully constrained sketch history. FreeCAD itself was not run here, so GUI
execution and FCStd saving remain untested in native FreeCAD.
For direct CAD access, open individual files in FreeCAD_STEP. Select one
solid, then File > Export > STL Mesh. Do not print the assembled reference.

ASSEMBLY AND USE
1. Place both cradles with feet flat on the desk and an inner gap of 180 mm.
2. Seat a bar into both lower sockets from the reading side. Repeat with the
   upper sockets. Keep the front faces flush or slightly recessed. No screws.
3. Center the book with both covers on the ledges. The 180 mm backrests support
   the lower portion of its stiff covers. Check for cover flex and binding strain.
4. Insert each page finger in the first slot ahead of its page stack. Lift it
   about 14 mm along the page to remove it, turn the page, then replace it.
5. Smooth paper-contact edges, and use a level, non-slip surface.

LOAD CHECK
The requested 8 lb book is a design target, not a tested rating. Before using
the textbook, test at floor level with padded loads, gradually increasing to
the intended weight while preventing a fall. Inspect for sliding, bending,
loose joints, cracks, or layer separation. Stop if any appear. Stay nearby
during an initial one-hour test and inspect during early reading sessions.
Remove the book between sessions. Keep PLA away from heat and direct sun.
Remove the book before moving the stand and lift beneath both bases.

DIGITAL VALIDATION
Every exported piece is a valid single CAD solid and a closed, consistently
wound STL mesh. STEP exports reimported with negligible geometric difference.
Assembled pieces do not intersect. Sampled connector insertion paths and all
seven keeper slots were checked. Connector mating regions match compact V2.
The macro geometry was run through an OpenCascade adapter. These checks do
not validate the strength or tolerances of a physical PLA print.
