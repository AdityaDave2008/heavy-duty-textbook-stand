# Assemble the V3 stand

Print one left cradle, one right cradle, two connector bars, and two page fingers. All connections use printed parts.

1. **Check the joints.** Print the [fit coupons](../stl/fit_tests/) and check that the connector key seats and the page finger slides in and lifts out.
2. **Print the cradles.** Place one cradle on each plate with its broad inner frame face down. The V3 starting settings for ordinary PLA are 0.24 mm layers, six walls, seven top and bottom layers, 25% gyroid infill, and a 5 mm brim. Use the supplied scale and orientation.
3. **Print the four small parts.** For the bars and fingers, the original print guide specifies 0.20 mm layers, six walls, and 100% infill.
4. **Join the cradles.** Place both feet flat with a 180 mm gap between the inner faces. From the reading side, seat one bar in the lower sockets and the other in the upper sockets. Their front faces should sit flush or slightly recessed.
5. **Position the page fingers.** Center the open book across both ledges. Insert each finger into the first slot ahead of its page stack. Lift it about 14 mm along the page to turn a sheet, then replace it.

The [original V3 print and assembly instructions](PLA_Print_and_Assembly.txt) cover fit tolerances, bridge inspection, and plate-specific settings. See the [load-test plan](load_test.md) for the 8 lb target.
